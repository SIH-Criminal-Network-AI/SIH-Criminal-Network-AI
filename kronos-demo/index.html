KRONOS INTELLIGENCE LAYER

How the network is reconstructed

The 3D graph visualises the investigation structure: the centre represents the primary subject, the ring represents front companies, the small dots represent accounts, and the outer nodes represent overseas entities. Pick a step to highlight that layer.

Multi-source AI fusion

  • FIRs, CDRs, financial records, surveillance, social media and intelligence are read together, not one by one.
  • NLP, vision and speech recognition pull out events and entities, then link the same person or company across documents.
  • Agentic AI plans which source to check next and correlates the results.

Network and relationship mapping

  • A knowledge graph shows who is connected to whom: associates, financiers, intermediaries.
  • Community detection finds the six companies as one cluster; centrality analysis points to the kingpin.
  • Hidden link prediction suggests connections nobody has recorded yet.

Temporal and anomaly detection

  • Impossible travel: the same identity in two places faster than anyone could travel.
  • Sequence detection spots repeat patterns, such as a call followed by a deposit followed by a shipment.
  • Unusual account behaviour across the 56 accounts is flagged for review.

Evidence-linked prioritisation

  • A hypothesis engine generates leads, ranks them and gives a confidence score.
  • Every lead links to the exact records behind it, so an investigator can verify it.
  • The Next Best Evidence list tells the team what to collect next.

Human-in-the-loop: the AI suggests, the investigator decides. Federated learning and blockchain keep an audit trail and protect data across agencies.

Projected impact

  • Narcotics units: 3–6 months earlier detection, 40–60% less investigation time and cost.
  • Frontline police: 50–70% less time to actionable leads, 2–4x more linked entities per case.
  • MHA: 2–3x stronger inter-agency collaboration.

These are our projected estimates from the idea submission, not measured results.

EVIDENCE SOURCES

Datasets and sources

Real police data is sensitive and not public, so the prototype is built on public and verified sources plus synthetic data. Open each source to see how we use it.

FIR records

Actual FIRs collected from verified sources. Multilingual text is read with OCR and NLP, then turned into people, places and events.

Court hearings

Court records and hearings, including cases from the National Judicial Data Grid, give criminal history and links between cases.

CDR and financial transactions

Not available publicly, so we use synthetic data shaped like real call and bank records to test impossible-travel and money-trail detection.

Social media and surveillance reports

Public social media posts and sample surveillance-style reports feed the fusion step.

Open government data

data.gov.in and Narcotics Control Bureau figures give the national enforcement trends used in our impact charts (2020–2024).
